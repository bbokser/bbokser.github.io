Hi! I'm Ben Bokser. I love making novel robots and building the vast, multidisciplinary skill set required to do so.

I currently work on the [UMV](https://youtu.be/6wQgpGYvG10) project at [RAI Institute](https://rai-inst.com/). Before that, I worked at the [Robotic Exploration Lab](https://roboticexplorationlab.org/) while getting my master's degree at Carnegie Mellon, and before that, I worked at [Liberating Technologies](https://www.liberatingtech.com/).

About

Hi! I’m Ben Bokser. I love making novel robots and building the vast, multidisciplinary skill set required to do so.

I currently work on the UMV project at RAI Institute. Before that, I worked at the Robotic Exploration Lab while getting my master’s degree at Carnegie Mellon, and before that, I worked at Liberating Technologies.
